Business workflow automation

Multi-store stock management SaaS

A custom answer to a business need the client's product didn't cover: distributing stock across several points of sale. The application ingests each store's stock and sales (CSV/Excel import), computes a target distribution per product based on sales and flags imbalances store by store (stock-outs, overstock). It then suggests the transfers to make and generates print-ready PDF transfer slips, fully configurable (columns, sorting, grouping by route or by sending store). Designed and deployed end to end autonomously: Django, dockerized, 116 tests, product variant handling, per-company roles and access.

PythonDjangoPostgreSQLDockerPDF generation116 tests
Result Built quickly, it enabled the client to win a major customer they could not have served without this multi-store workflow automation capability.
